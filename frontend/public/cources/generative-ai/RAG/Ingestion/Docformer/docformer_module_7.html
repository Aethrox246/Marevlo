Document AI Masterclass

Module Goal:

By the end of this module, you will see exactly how a general-purpose, pretrained DocFormer brain gets turned into specialized, high-value business tools. You’ll master the three most common downstream tasks (sequence labeling, receipt parsing, document classification), understand their datasets, real-world applications, and — most importantly — learn how to attach task-specific heads and fine-tune the model for production use.

From General Brain → Specialized Expert

Pretraining gives DocFormer broad document intelligence.

Fine-tuning narrows that intelligence to solve concrete, money-making problems.

Here are the three flagship downstream tasks that power most production Document AI systems today.
