Document AI Masterclass

Module Goal:

By the end of this module, you will understand exactly how DocFormer becomes intelligent about millions of diverse documents without expensive human labeling. You’ll master the three clever self-supervised “games” that force the model to discover deep patterns in text, layout, and visuals, turning raw unlabeled scans into a powerful, general-purpose document brain.

Why Self-Supervised Pretraining?

Labeling every invoice, receipt, form, and table in the world is impossible.

Instead, DocFormer plays self-supervised games, tasks where the data itself provides the supervision.

The model has to “guess” hidden parts of the document using the other visible parts → forcing multi-modal reasoning.
